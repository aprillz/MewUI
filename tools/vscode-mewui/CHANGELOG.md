# Change Log

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Preview any `Window` or `UserControl` with a parameterless constructor, plus the app's main window as your entry point built it.
- Every save updates the preview through .NET Hot Reload, without restarting the app.
- Mouse, wheel, keyboard, and typed text on the preview canvas reach the running app.
- Target picker, light/dark toggle, zoom (Fit to 200%), refresh, and session restart in the panel toolbar.
- The preview follows the active editor file, and picking a target jumps to its declaration.
- Windows, Linux, and macOS.
